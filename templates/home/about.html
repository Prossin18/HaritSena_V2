🌿 About HaritSena

About Us

HaritSena is a community-driven platform that connects citizens, waste collectors and industries to create a cleaner, greener and healthier environment for everyone.

Join HaritSena

Our Story

HaritSena was founded with a simple belief – every small step towards responsible waste management can lead to a big change.

We bridge the gap between citizens who want to dispose of waste responsibly and waste collectors who work every day to keep our cities clean.

🎯

Our Mission

To promote responsible waste management and build a sustainable environment for future generations.

👁️

Our Vision

A clean, green and sustainable India where waste is managed efficiently and everyone contributes to a better tomorrow.

👥

Our Values

  • Resposibility
  • Sustainability
  • Transparency
  • Community
  • Trust
